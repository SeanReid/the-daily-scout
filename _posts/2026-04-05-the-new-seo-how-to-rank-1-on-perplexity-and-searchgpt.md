Google traffic is tanking. If you've looked at your analytics lately, you've probably noticed the slow, painful bleed of organic clicks. But people haven't stopped searching—they've just moved. They are asking Perplexity, SearchGPT, and Claude.

Traditional SEO was about backlink profiles, exact-match keywords, and gaming the algorithm. The New SEO—Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)—is entirely about entity relationships, information gain, and authoritative citations. AI search engines don't care about your keyword density; they care if your content provides the most direct, highly-cited answer to a user's prompt.

To rank #1 on Perplexity and SearchGPT, you need to produce content with extreme velocity, deep research, and perfect formatting. You are no longer writing for human skimmers first; you are formatting data for Large Language Models to parse, which then summarize it for humans. 

After completely overhauling my own content engine and testing dozens of workflow setups, I realized that adapting to AI search isn't just a software game—it requires a physical desk setup built for intense, multi-window research and rapid AI prompting.
